# Q3 Financial Performance Report
**SortPilot Technologies, Inc.**
Date: October 2024 | Prepared by: Finance & Strategy

## Executive Summary
During the third quarter of 2024, SortPilot demonstrated significant growth across key business segments. Total consolidated revenue reached **$4.2 million**, representing a **32% year-over-year increase** compared to Q3 2023 ($3.18 million). Net income for the period stood at **$840,000**, with an operating margin of **20.0%**.

## Key Revenue Drivers
1. **Enterprise Local AI Subscriptions**: Generated $2.6M (62% of total revenue), driven by expanding deployments in legal, financial, and healthcare sectors requiring 100% on-premise privacy.
2. **Professional Services & Integration**: Generated $1.1M, supporting on-premise hardware cluster installations and fine-tuning local models.
3. **Developer API & Platform Licensing**: Generated $500K, reflecting a 45% sequential quarter growth.

## Operating Expenses & Investments
- **Research & Development**: $1.4M (invested heavily into local embedding indexers, quantization optimizations, and multi-modal file ingestion).
- **Sales & Marketing**: $1.1M.
- **General & Administrative**: $860K.

## Full-Year Guidance & Outlook
For fiscal year 2024, SortPilot raises its annual revenue guidance to **$16.5M - $17.2M**, up from the previous forecast of $15.0M. Key priorities for Q4 include launching local edge assistants for desktop workstations and enhancing zero-cloud enterprise compliance.
